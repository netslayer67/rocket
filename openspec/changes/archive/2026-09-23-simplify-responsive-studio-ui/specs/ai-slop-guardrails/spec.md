## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Simplified workflow review
The dashboard SHALL record the anti-slop, contrast, keyboard, narrow-viewport, overflow, and reduced-motion review whenever hierarchy is simplified through native disclosures or compact status layouts. The review MUST confirm that primary actions are not hidden, optional disclosures state their purpose, and no new decorative surface, icon system, gradient, glow, dependency, or synthetic activity is introduced.

#### Scenario: Simplified view is accepted
- **WHEN** the Studio or monitoring hierarchy is reorganized
- **THEN** the review records rendered phone and desktop checks, keyboard disclosure use, reduced-motion behavior, and the simpler alternative selected
